Twain · two other ways to play

Not a card game.
Same seven whys.

Both mockups work. Tap through each one as Anthony and Maya.

Mode A

Say it on three

The phone lies on the table between you. It shows a moment and counts you in, and you both say your answers out loud at the same time. The phone only keeps score.

Why it’s fun: no passing, no hiding, and the reveal happens at the same moment, out loud. It works at dinner, in the car, or with a group.

Mode B

Plan a free Saturday

There are no labels to pick. You make four honest choices about a free day, and each one quietly carries a why. Your partner predicts your day first.

Why it’s insightful: it watches what you choose instead of asking what you value. You see the whys only after you’ve chosen.
