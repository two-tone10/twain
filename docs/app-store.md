## Description
Keep is a 45-second game that shows you what you actually reach for.

Moments fall like stars: a slow coffee, dinner with old friends, a trail you’ve never tried. Tap the ones you’d keep. Your sky holds five. You don’t have time to think, and that’s the point. Your thumb answers before your inner critic does.

Every round draws a constellation with a name, like The Restless Hearth or The Quiet Forge. There are 49 to find. Play a few rounds and you’ll find your sign.

THEN MAKE ONE REAL
After a round, pick one star that could happen this week, say when and with whom, and Keep checks back to ask how it went. Over time, Kept shows everything you’ve done, who was there, and how what you reach for changes.

PLAY TOGETHER, ON YOUR OWN PHONES
• Today’s drop: the same stars for everyone, once a day.
• Match: send a drop to a friend. You each catch the same stars, guess three of the other’s five, and see how in sync you are.
• Circles: a small group that plays the daily together. See each other’s skies once you’ve played, guess each other’s picks, and get a weekly card.

MORE WAYS TO PLAY
• Drift: hold on to the stars you won’t let fade.
• Slow drop: one drop across a whole day.
• Decks for where you are: Everyday, Campus, New city, With kids, Next chapter, Young.

No account. No ads. Your history stays on your phone; only anonymous counts of what gets kept are shared.

## Review notes
No login is needed. Tap “Today’s drop”, then tap falling stars to keep five. The friend-challenge links open lipurposefe.com/keep/play.

Player content and safety (Guideline 1.2):
- There is no chat or free text between players. The only player content is short "stars" (moments, 30 characters), first names, and circle names.
- Stars are reviewed by a person before anyone else sees them. Names and circle names are filtered for links and offensive words.
- Report: after a drop, “Stars this round came from players · Report”, or press and hold one on the commons board. Three reports hide a star for everyone until reviewed.
- Report and block a person: the ••• button next to their name on a Match or in a Circle (“Hide”, or “Report and hide”). Hiding works both ways. Circle owners can remove members.
- Removed content disappears from every phone the next time the app opens. Contact: hello@lipurposefe.com.
- Data deletion: tap the round “You” button on Home → “Delete my data from Keep’s server” or “Erase everything”. There are no accounts.
